# Game Design Brief

**Status:** DRAFT — requires project-owner approval  
**Project:** FRONTLINE: LAST SIGNAL  
**Target platforms:** Android and Windows PC  
**Visual target:** High-quality cinematic presentation with balanced performance

## 1. Core concept

A compact, replayable multiplayer arena-survival experience set in an original coastal-industrial location. Players explore the environment, collect match resources, complete map objectives, and compete to earn the highest score / remain in the round.

This brief is a starting point, not a claim that these systems have been implemented.

## 2. Experience pillars

1. **Readable gameplay:** objectives, routes, pickups, and status are easy to understand.
2. **Strong atmosphere:** distinctive architecture, weather, lighting, and environmental storytelling.
3. **Responsive input:** touch controls on Android and keyboard/mouse on PC.
4. **Fair performance:** stable frame pacing and scalable quality settings.
5. **Original identity:** no copied maps, characters, branding, or proprietary assets.

## 3. Initial playable area

A small coastal-industrial test zone containing:
- One main warehouse exterior
- A limited number of secondary structures
- A road and loading area
- A shoreline and water boundary
- A few environmental props
- Clearly marked objective / collection locations

The first milestone is a polished test area, not a huge open world.

## 4. Prototype gameplay scope

Candidate systems for the first playable prototype:
- Main menu and settings
- Character movement and camera
- Touch and keyboard/mouse input
- Match timer and score
- Collection and objective interactions
- Round start, end, and restart
- Basic local test mode

Multiplayer networking, progression, cosmetics, and large maps are later milestones and must not be represented as complete until implemented and tested.

## 5. Visual direction

- Cinematic atmosphere with clear visual readability
- Physically based materials where supported by the chosen engine
- Distinct material response for concrete, painted metal, glass, asphalt, wood, soil, and vegetation
- Carefully controlled fog, sky, shadows, and reflections
- Mobile scalability: reduced effects and texture budgets where necessary

## 6. Open decisions

- Final engine selection
- Camera perspective and player presentation
- Match rules and player count
- Online networking architecture
- Minimum supported Android device
- PC minimum/recommended specifications
- Final art style reference board

All open decisions remain DRAFT until approved.
